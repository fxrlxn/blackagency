§ · Pricing FAQ

The money questions,
answered plainly.

Q1Is the monthly price really all-in?

Yes, the plan fee covers the scope we define together. The only variables are things like extra entities or unusually high transaction volume, which we agree on up front.

Q2Do you require an annual contract?

Never. Plans are month-to-month. We earn your business every month with the quality of our work.

Q3What if my needs change mid-year?

Move up or down a plan anytime. As you grow (or slow), your fee and scope adjust to match, no penalties.

Q4How does billing work?

Monthly via ACH or card on a fixed date. One-time projects like a books clean-up are quoted separately before we begin.
