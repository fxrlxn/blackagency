§ 01 · Our story

Two partners, one filing cabinet, and a stubborn belief that accounting could feel personal again.

When Eleanor Halden and James Roe left their big-firm partnerships in 2006, they were tired of watching clients become account numbers. So they built the opposite: a practice small enough that a senior CPA reviews every return, and confident enough to give straight answers instead of billable runarounds.

Eighteen years later, we've grown to a tight team of eleven, but the rule hasn't changed. Every client works with a partner-level accountant, every month, all year. That's the whole idea, and we guard it carefully.
