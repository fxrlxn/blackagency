What we do.

Six core services, one senior CPA on your account. Engage us for a single need or hand us the whole back office; either way, you get proactive, plain-spoken work.

01Tax

Tax Planning & Preparation

We treat tax as a year-round discipline, not a springtime scramble. Strategic planning lowers what you owe; meticulous preparation keeps you filed accurately and on time.

  • ✓Business & personal returns

    Federal, state, and multi-state filing for entities and their owners.

  • ✓Proactive tax strategy

    Quarterly planning to time income, deductions, and distributions in your favor.

  • ✓IRS correspondence & representation

    We handle notices and stand in for you if questions arise.

02Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping & Monthly Close

Clean, current books are the foundation of every good decision. We categorize, reconcile, and close every month, then hand you reports that are actually readable.

  • ✓Monthly reconciliation & close

    Bank, card, and loan accounts tied out every period.

  • ✓Clear financial statements

    P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow you can read at a glance.

  • ✓Catch-up & clean-up

    Behind on the books? We'll get months (or years) back in order.

§ · Questions

Before you ask,
you probably want to know…

Q1Do you work with businesses outside Massachusetts?

Yes. We serve clients across New England and remotely nationwide. Multi-state filing and out-of-state entities are routine for us.

Q2What accounting software do you use?

We work primarily in QuickBooks Online and Xero, and can adapt to most modern stacks. If you're on spreadsheets, we'll migrate you cleanly.

Q3Can I start with just one service?

Absolutely. Many clients begin with tax or a books clean-up, then add services as they grow. There's no all-or-nothing requirement.

Q4How are you different from a big firm?

You get a senior CPA who knows your business, not a rotating cast of juniors. Flat pricing, direct access, and proactive advice year-round.
